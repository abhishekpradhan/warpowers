THE PEOPLE AND PROJECTS BEHIND THE BATTLEFIELD

Credits & licenses

War Powers combines original game content with the work of open-source communities. Its code, content and dependencies retain their respective licenses.

The engine

Built on GeneralsX and the GPL-released Command & Conquer: Generals engine lineage, with contributions from TheSuperHackers and the community porting projects. The engine is GPL-3.0 with Electronic Arts’ additional terms. No EA game assets or game data are included.

The browser renderer uses dvijoke by Meerzulee (MIT). The native development build uses DXVK (zlib). The browser build uses DirectX headers supplied through DXVK, with their separate notices below; it does not use the native DXVK renderer.

Art, sound & tools

Original War Powers models, textures, maps, interface artwork, effects and processed radio voices are authored through the project’s reproducible pipelines. Blender and OpenSAGE.BlenderPlugin support modeling and W3D export. Quaternius’s CC0 models helped the prototype; the current vehicle designs are original replacements. Liberation Sans is by Red Hat and the Liberation Fonts contributors, under SIL OFL 1.1.

Music

Music by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com): “Volatile Reaction”, “Interloper”, “Crypto”, “Rites”, “Mechanolith”, and “Stormfront”. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Tracks are re-encoded for browser delivery; “Stormfront” is trimmed with a fade.
